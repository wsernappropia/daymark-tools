Frequently asked questions

How does the trial work?
You can use Invoice Filing Assistant for 14 days or 30 completed filings, whichever comes first. The trial starts when the extension is first used on that device. A reminder appears when 3 days or 5 filings remain. After the trial ends, you need a license to keep filing. If Chrome sync is turned on, the trial start time and the number of filings are stored in Chrome sync so a reinstall does not reset the trial.

What does the license cost?
A one-time USD 12 license, usable on up to two devices. Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record. If a licensed device cannot reach Dodo Payments, the license stays valid for 48 hours.

How do I enter a license key?
After the trial ends, the popup shows a paywall with a Buy link and a “Have a key?” link. “Have a key?” focuses the license field so you can paste your key and click Activate. During the trial, the same license field is already visible in the popup. You can also open the options page (the ••• button in the popup) to confirm the installed version.

Where do I get a receipt or ask for a refund?
Receipts and refund requests are handled through Dodo Payments. If you need help, email us and we will take it up with Dodo.

How do I contact you?
Email daymarktools@proton.me. Never attach a real invoice.

Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm Chrome is current.
  2. Record the extension version from chrome://extensions or from the options page.
  3. Reproduce with harmless sample data when possible.
  4. Never send passwords, card data, one-time codes, private documents or real invoice content.

Safe test

Use our harmless sample invoice to verify the filing flow without exposing personal or business data. A welcome tab with the same test link opens on first install.

Product limits

  • Invoice Filing Assistant saves within Chrome’s Downloads directory and supported subfolders, under Invoices/year-month.
  • It can file a PDF that is open directly, linked as a .pdf URL, or served as application/pdf even when the link does not end in .pdf.
  • Suggested names come from the filename and URL, not from reading PDF contents. Dates may be year-month-day, day-month-year, or month names. The filename includes currency when it is known.
  • Optional auto-renaming, when enabled, applies to PDF downloads without a review step, and uses the same trial or license as filing from the popup. “File a PDF from this page” is the flow that lets you review and edit details first.
  • The local history is a list you can browse, filter to items that need review, and export as JSON; it is not a searchable backup.
