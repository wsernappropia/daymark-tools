Frequently asked questions

How does the trial work?
You can use Invoice Filing Assistant for 14 days or 30 completed filings, whichever comes first. The trial starts when the extension is first used on that device. After it ends, you need a license to keep filing.

What does the license cost?
A one-time USD 12 license, usable on up to two devices. Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record.

How do I enter a license key?
Open the extension popup, paste your key into the license field, and click Activate. The same popup has the buy link once checkout is live.

Where do I get a receipt or ask for a refund?
Receipts and refund requests are handled through Dodo Payments. If you need help, email us and we will take it up with Dodo.

How do I contact you?
Email daymarktools@proton.me. Never attach a real invoice.

Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm Chrome is current.
  2. Record the extension version from chrome://extensions.
  3. Reproduce with harmless sample data when possible.
  4. Never send passwords, card data, one-time codes, private documents or real invoice content.

Safe test

Use our harmless sample invoice to verify the filing flow without exposing personal or business data.

Product limits

  • Invoice Filing Assistant saves within Chrome’s Downloads directory and supported subfolders.
  • It can only find a PDF that is open directly or linked from the current page with a URL that ends in .pdf.
  • Suggested names come from the filename and URL, not from reading PDF contents.
  • Optional auto-renaming, when enabled, applies to PDF downloads without a review step. “File a PDF from this page” is the flow that lets you review and edit details first.
  • The local history is a list you can browse and export as JSON; it is not a searchable backup.
